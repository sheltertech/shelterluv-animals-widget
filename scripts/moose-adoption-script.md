## Main video script (~60–90 seconds)

**[OPEN — hook, direct to camera or over b-roll of Moose]**

Moose has been at Jelly's Place for **842 days**.

That's more than two years waiting for a home — and he's still here.

**[CUT — Moose playing fetch, at the beach, or walking on leash]**

Meet Moose. Some folks at the shelter know him as Alex. He's a four-year-old chocolate Lab — smart, athletic, and absolutely obsessed with fetch.

He loves the beach. He loves car rides — he can barely contain himself when he knows an adventure is coming. He walks beautifully on a leash and checks in with you constantly. People say he's one of the smartest dogs they've met. He knows tricks, and he'd probably crush agility, Frisbee, or nose work.

**[CUT — calmer moment; honest tone]**

Moose isn't perfect — and that's okay. He can get nervous when strangers try to pet him. He doesn't like his feet touched, which makes nail trims tricky. Some hard things happened when he was younger, and he still carries that with him.

Kids scare him. He needs an adult-only home with a yard, no cats, and someone who understands that trust takes time.

**[CUT — Moose being affectionate with a familiar person]**

But when he knows you? He's gentle. Playful. Obedient. Affectionate. Crate trained. He even likes to swim.

He's neutered, microchipped, and up to date on vaccines. His adoption fee is reduced — because after 842 days, he more than deserves a second chance.

**[CLOSE — CTA on screen]**

If Moose sounds like your guy, apply at **jellysplace.org**.

Share this. Tag a friend. Let's get Moose home.

**Subject:** 842 days — Moose still needs a home

Hi,

Moose (also known as Alex at Jelly's Place) is a four-year-old chocolate Labrador who has been waiting for adoption since May 2024 — **842 days and counting**.

He's an active, intelligent dog who excels at fetch, loves the beach and car rides, and walks beautifully on leash. He'd be a fantastic partner for Bay Area adventures, agility, or nose work.

Moose needs a patient, adult-only household with a yard. He is not suited for homes with children or cats. He can be nervous around strangers and is sensitive about having his feet touched, but he is affectionate and loyal with people he trusts.

Moose is neutered, microchipped, current on vaccines, crate trained, and available with a **reduced adoption fee**.

Meet him: apply at [jellysplace.org](https://jellysplace.org)  
Animal ID: JELP-A-1821

Thank you for sharing his story.
